About Farmigo

Farmigo uses advanced AI technology to help farmers identify plant diseases and provide expert agricultural advice. Upload photos of your plants or describe symptoms to get instant analysis and recommendations.

This application is developed by the students of KLH University.

We aim to empower farmers with the knowledge and tools they need to maintain healthy crops and maximize yields.

Our AI model is trained on a vast dataset of plant diseases and agricultural practices, ensuring accurate and reliable results.
